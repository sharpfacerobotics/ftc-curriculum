Design tools

Check the design before you cut

16 calculators and checkers for the numbers that decide whether a mechanism works, plus a file check that reads your exported CAD and measures it.

CAD file checkDrop in a STEP or STL export and see which of the exercise’s numbers your model actually hit.Arm gravity torqueWork the torque an arm needs at its worst angle, then the reduction that delivers it.Linear slide sizingExtension, spool diameter, and the force the cable really carries.Arm simulatorRun a time-stepped arm against your own numbers and watch it fail or hold.Tap drill and clearanceThe hole sizes that stop a screw binding, straight from the standards.Weight budgetSpend the robot’s mass on purpose instead of discovering it at inspection.

A calculator checks the arithmetic behind a design. Whether the mechanism grips the game element is what a prototype is for.
