# Unit 12 Comprehensive Coding Challenge

The starter is an empty TeleOp with the required IMU and drivetrain imports.

## Your Task

1. Map the IMU and initialize it with the Control Hub's physical logo and USB orientation.
2. Map four drive motors and make their directions consistent.
3. Read yaw, pitch, and roll from `YawPitchRollAngles` in a named `AngleUnit`.
4. Let the driver reset yaw with a button.
5. Rotate joystick input with sine and cosine for field-centric control.
6. Calculate and normalize four mecanum wheel powers.
7. Detect a dangerous pitch or roll angle and stop or reduce motion.
8. Include a target-heading error or correction term and report orientation through telemetry.

Keep all angle units explicit. Degrees and radians must not be mixed accidentally.
